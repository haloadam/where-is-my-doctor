# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the people the project serves.

Unacceptable behaviour includes harassment, insulting or derogatory comments, personal or political
attacks, and publishing others' private information without permission.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported to the project
maintainer at **adam.olah@adroitgroup.io**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly
and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.
